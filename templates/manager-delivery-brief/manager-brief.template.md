# Manager Delivery Brief

> Layout guidance only. The deliverable must be generated as the canonical projection of `manager-brief.json`.

Lead with current delivery, reusable asset left behind, observable before/after change, human challenge, evidence boundary, known limitations, next-use path, and one narrow manager decision.

Every material statement must map to the structured brief as `OBSERVED`, `DERIVED`, `JUDGED`, or `UNKNOWN`. Validate with `--rendered-brief`; hand-edited divergence is rejected.
