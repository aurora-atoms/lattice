# Manager-Ready Delivery Brief

> Template only. State the evidence origin and whether this is synthetic before any claim.

Lead with current delivery, reusable asset left behind, observable before/after change, human challenge, evidence boundary, known limitations, next-use path, and one narrow manager decision.

Every material statement must map to the structured brief as `OBSERVED`, `DERIVED`, `JUDGED`, or `UNKNOWN`. Do not strengthen the structured claim, omit limitations, or present synthetic conformance as real use.
